# Design

## Art direction

Use the `design-taste-frontend` skill for art direction and the house style. Read it before you design or restyle a page.

## Design loop

After you build a page or screen, read [Design loop](loop/index.md) and run it: score the design, fix it and score it again until it is good, then do a performance pass.
